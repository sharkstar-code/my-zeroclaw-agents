Is AI a Threat? — Royal Institute of Philosophy

TL;DR A provocative evening of philosophical interrogation on AI's existential stakes, hosted by one of London's most intellectually rigorous institutions—not a tech-industry cheerleading session.

Why it Matters

This isn't your typical "AI ethics" panel designed to make tech executives feel virtuous. The Royal Institute of Philosophy has a 160-year track record of rigorous philosophical inquiry. An evening framed as "Is AI a threat?" signals they're not interested in false balance or techno-optimism. This is the place where serious philosophers actually interrogate the conceptual foundations of what we're building and whether we should be building it. The format—two thought-provoking talks followed by interactive breakout discussions—creates real intellectual friction rather than performative debate.

Where and When

Swedenborg Hall, London
Thursday, May 14, 2026 | 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM (18:00-21:00)

Book tickets via Eventbrite. Free or nominal fee expected. The venue itself—Swedenborg Hall—carries philosophical weight; it's a space historically associated with esoteric and philosophical inquiry, not corporate auditoriums.

Access & Logistics

  • In-person attendance only
  • First-come, first-served seating
  • Arrive early for discussion breakouts
  • Highly likely to attract serious philosophers, technologists with genuine doubts, and intellectuals skeptical of AI hype

Insider Note: This is where you'll find the actual conversation about AI's philosophical implications, not the sanitised corporate version. The Royal Institute's centenary lectures series ("Philosophy in Retrospect and Prospect") signals they're in a reflective, critical mode. Perfect for anyone who finds typical AI discourse intellectually insulting.
